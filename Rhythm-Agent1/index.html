I1 · Learning from Film

Rich film-transition data taught our models distinctive video orchestration and a cinematic sense. Intelligence is a higher-level abstraction of data; rich data and simple SFT can go a long way.

I2 · A Thin management of Tool-learning

When model capabilities fall short, a thin shell packages complex multimodal interactions as tools: video understanding, music beat extraction, editing, and even the video-understanding workflow itself.

I3 · Scaling the works

We explored scaling Qwen-VL models from 7B to 72B, alongside larger Qwen text models at 35B and 235B.

I4 · Multimodality alone is not intelligence

Multimodality alone is not intelligence — thinking and reasoning are.

With a strong foundation model, image-understanding and audio-feature extraction tools, and a little guidance, the model can even use dynamic programming to orchestrate video and capture higher-level patterns in rhythm. Compute wins again.
